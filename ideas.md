# Asthetik Dental — Design Brainstorm

<idea>
**Design Movement:** Clinical Luxury / Contemporary Medical Premium
**Core Principles:**
1. Deep navy (#0B1F3A) as the dominant structural color — conveys trust, authority, and professionalism
2. Gold/amber (#C9A84C) as the accent — signals premium quality and warmth
3. Pure white backgrounds for content sections — clinical cleanliness
4. Generous whitespace with tight typographic hierarchy

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy evokes the trust of medical authority; gold accents signal that this is not a budget clinic but a premium destination. Together they communicate "world-class care in your city."

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric split-screen sections — hero uses a 60/40 split with text left and image right. Alternating left/right image-text pairs for service sections. Full-bleed dark sections break the rhythm.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin gold horizontal rule separators between sections
2. Circular badge icons with navy fill and gold border for trust indicators
3. Card hover states with gold left-border reveal

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth, unhurried transitions (300ms ease-out). Cards lift slightly on hover. Scroll-triggered fade-in for section entries.

**Animation:** Fade-up entrance animations for section headings; staggered card reveals; sticky nav with subtle shadow on scroll.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (headings) + Poppins (body). Heading hierarchy: 56px/bold hero, 36px/semibold section titles, 18px/medium card titles, 15px/regular body.

**Core Principles:**
1. Off-white (#F8F9FA) base with teal (#0D7377) primary — calm, hygienic, modern
2. Extreme whitespace discipline — every element earns its place
3. Monospaced accents for data/stats to feel precise and clinical

**Layout Paradigm:** Single-column centered content with max-width 760px for text, full-bleed for images. Strict 8px grid. Sections separated by generous padding only.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin teal underlines on section headings
2. Large background numbers (opacity 5%) behind section titles
3. Pill-shaped tags for treatment categories

**Interaction Philosophy:** Micro-interactions only — subtle color fills on hover, no dramatic transforms. Keyboard-accessible focus states.

**Animation:** Minimal — only opacity fades, no movement. Content should feel still and authoritative.

**Typography System:** DM Sans (headings) + Source Sans Pro (body). Clean, geometric, highly legible.
